KONAK HOME

KONAK HOME is a personal home automation and camera recording project based on Home Assistant. It is intended for use by its owner, not as a public camera service.

Google Drive integration

The integration uses rclone and Google OAuth to back up authorized camera recordings to the owner's Google Drive account and retrieve them for playback or download. Access is limited to the files authorized through the requested Google Drive file permission.

Camera recordings and account credentials are not published on this informational website.

For details about data access, use, storage and deletion, read the Privacy Policy.
