Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 November 2026

This policy describes the intended personal-use KONAK HOME integration and its handling of Google user data.

Data accessed

The Google Drive integration requests the drive.file permission for authorized files. It handles camera recordings, associated file names, identifiers, timestamps, sizes, integrity checksums and storage quota information as needed to manage backups. OAuth credentials and tokens are used for authorization.

How data is used

Google user data is used to upload, verify, retrieve, play back, download and manage authorized recordings. The integration is not intended for advertising, sale of personal data, profiling or training generalized AI models.

Storage and security

Recordings may be stored on the owner's Home Assistant server and in the authorized Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are intended to remain in the owner's local rclone configuration. The public website does not request Google account access or publish recordings or credentials. Google and GitHub operate their respective services under their own policies.

Sharing

The integration is intended for personal use and does not intentionally disclose Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the requested functionality through Google Drive or as required by law. Access should be restricted to authorized users.

Retention and deletion

Recordings are retained according to the owner's archive settings and available storage. The owner can delete recordings through the archive or Google Drive, subject to the applicable trash and retention settings. Locked recordings are intended to be excluded from automatic cleanup. The owner can revoke OAuth access at Google Account connections and remove locally stored credentials. Revoking access does not itself delete previously stored recordings.

Google API compliance

KONAK HOME's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project maintainer via the project issue tracker. Do not post private recordings, credentials or personal details in public issues.
